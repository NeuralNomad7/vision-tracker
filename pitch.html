The Problem

Robots are blind without reliable perception.

To interact with the physical world, robots need real-time, low-latency object tracking they can trust. Existing computer vision solutions are expensive, fragile under real-world conditions, or introduce latency that makes closed-loop control impossible. The gap between "works in the lab" and "works on the factory floor" remains enormous.

Our Solution

Lightweight. Production-hardened.
Runs on commodity hardware.

Vision Tracker delivers Kalman-filtered, multi-object tracking with sub-pixel accuracy over a standard webcam. No GPU required. No cloud dependency. Just deterministic, real-time perception that streams target vectors directly to your robot controller.

  • OpenCV + Kalman Filtering
  • Sub-pixel Accuracy
  • 30 Hz Deterministic Output
  • Zero Cloud Dependencies
  • Commodity USB Cameras
  • Plug-and-Play Serial

Capabilities

Three pillars of reliable perception.

🎯

Multi-Object Tracking

Track up to 10 color-segmented objects simultaneously with persistent IDs, HSV calibration, and distance estimation across the full frame.

⚡

Predictive Filtering

4-state Kalman filter predicts object trajectories through occlusions and noise, maintaining lock even when vision momentarily fails.

🤖

Robot Integration

Streams target vectors at 30 Hz over serial with sub-millisecond precision. CSV logging captures every data point for post-hoc analysis.

Reliability

Built for production, not just demos.

Vision Tracker is engineered for environments where downtime costs money. Every failure mode has a recovery path. Every edge case has a handler.
